Great Britain and Northern Ireland use different markets.

This GB page covers Great Britain’s market signal. Northern Ireland is part of the all-island Single Electricity Market with Ireland, so do not use GB as a Northern Ireland retail reference. Ofgem’s regional retail rates and standing charges are also distinct from the market-price signal shown here. Economy 7 hours also need checking with your supplier; the signal does not redefine them.

Sources: Ofgem — Economy 7 · Ofgem — rates and standing charges · SEMO — Ireland and Northern Ireland · Octopus — Agile tariff

Before you run an appliance

Does the price cap equal the number on this page?

No. The price cap concerns eligible default tariffs. The current GB source supplied by Elecz is an Octopus Agile reference, not the price cap or a tariff applicable to every household.

Will Economy 7 be cheapest for everyone?

No. The result depends on how much you use in each band and the rates and charges on your offer. Check your own pattern rather than assuming that seven cheaper hours guarantee a cheaper annual bill.

Should I pick GB for Northern Ireland?

No. This page is for Great Britain. The Ireland page explains the shared wholesale market, but neither page calculates your Northern Ireland supplier’s tariff.

Can the tool schedule an exact continuous charging block?

It can compare energy or power and runtime with available data. It does not guarantee a continuous block of exactly your requested duration and does not control the charger.
