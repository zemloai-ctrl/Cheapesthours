Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

Overview

CheapestHours.com ("CheapestHours", "we", "us") is a free tool that helps visitors compare wholesale electricity market prices and find cheaper times to use electricity. This page explains what data is collected when you visit the site and how it is used.

Data we collect

CheapestHours does not require an account, and we do not collect names, email addresses, or payment information. The site stores one thing in your browser's local storage: your last selected country, so the site remembers it on your next visit. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Cookies and advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertising. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or by visiting www.aboutads.info.

As a Google AdSense partner site, we follow the Google EU user consent policy where applicable. If you are visiting from the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, Google may show you a consent request before ads are personalized.

Analytics

We may use basic, privacy-respecting web analytics (such as aggregate visit and page-view counts) to understand how the site is used. This data is anonymized or aggregated and is not used to identify individual visitors.

Third-party data source

Live electricity market prices shown on CheapestHours are provided by Elecz, an independent electricity price signal API. No personal data is shared with Elecz as part of this integration — only anonymous requests for market price data.

Children's privacy

CheapestHours is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent via our Contact page.
