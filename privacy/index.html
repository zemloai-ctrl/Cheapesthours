Privacy Policy

2026-10-30

This policy explains how CheapestHours.com processes personal data. CheapestHours is a service of SKA-Trading Oy and uses electricity price data from Elecz, another service of the same company.

1. Controller

SKA-Trading Oy Business ID 1010164-9 Domicile Kokkola, Finland Privacy contact: privacy@elecz.com

2. What we process and why

Your selections. Choices are carried in page URLs and may remain in browser history and shared links. The country, region, appliance and usage choices you make are used to show when electricity is cheapest. We do not ask for your name, email address or other contact details.

Saved country. Your chosen country is saved in your browser's local storage (key cheapesthours-country) so you don't need to select it again. It stays on your device and you can clear it in your browser settings.

Price data. Electricity prices are fetched from the Elecz API. Elecz logs the request with a truncated IP address, as described in the Elecz privacy policy. Currency conversions may be fetched from the Frankfurter exchange rate service, which receives your IP address with the request.

Website use. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which collects aggregated usage data such as the page viewed, referring page, browser type and country, without cookies.

Server logs. Cloudflare records technical logs that may include IP address, time and requested URL, for security and troubleshooting.

Contacting us. If you email us, we process your name, email address and message to reply to you.

3. Advertising

CheapestHours includes Google AdSense advertising code. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Ads may be personalised based on your interests if you consent.

Visitors in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message (Funding Choices) before advertising cookies are used. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time through the privacy settings link at the bottom of the page.

Google acts as an independent controller for its advertising processing. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

4. Legal basis

Advertising cookies and personalised advertising are based on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). Other processing is based on our legitimate interest in providing, securing and improving the service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

6. Retention

The saved country stays in your browser until you clear it. Advertising cookies follow Google's retention periods. Emails are kept as long as needed to handle the matter and meet accounting obligations.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict processing, to object to processing based on legitimate interest, and to withdraw consent at any time. Send requests to privacy@elecz.com.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Finland this is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman. You may also contact the authority in your own country.
