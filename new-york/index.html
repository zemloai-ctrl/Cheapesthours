When is electricity cheapest in New York?

Wholesale electricity prices change throughout the day. CheapestHours uses live market data to show the current price and the cheapest hours coming up, helping you decide whether to use electricity now or wait.

The price shown here is the NYISO wholesale market price for the selected zone — not your final utility bill. Your actual rate can include delivery charges, capacity costs, taxes, and your utility's or supplier's markup, so it will typically be higher than the wholesale number.

This can be useful for flexible electricity use like EV charging, dishwashers, laundry, water heating, and other loads that can be shifted to a cheaper time — especially if you're on a real-time or wholesale-linked pricing plan.
