CheapestHours is maintained by SKA Trading Oy / Zemlo AI in Finland. We welcome questions about the service, corrections and reports of electricity-market data that appears stale or incorrect.

When reporting a data issue, please include the market or country, the approximate time you saw the problem and, if possible, the price or message shown on the page. Please do not include passwords, payment details or other sensitive information. That makes it much easier to distinguish a source-data outage from a display problem.

You can also contact us about privacy, accessibility, advertising or partnership questions. Electricity-market signals used by the tool are supplied by Elecz; more information about how CheapestHours uses them is available on our About & methodology page.
