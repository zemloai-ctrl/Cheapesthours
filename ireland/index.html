When is electricity cheapest in Ireland?

Electricity prices can change throughout the day. CheapestHours uses live wholesale market data to show the current price and the cheapest available hours, helping you decide whether to use electricity now or wait.

This can be useful for flexible electricity use such as EV charging, dishwashers, washing machines, dryers, water heating and other loads that can be moved to a cheaper time.

The prices shown here are wholesale market signals, not your final household tariff. Your actual electricity bill can also include supplier charges, network fees, taxes and the terms of your electricity plan.
